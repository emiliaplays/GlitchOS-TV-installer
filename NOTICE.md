# Project and third-party materials

GlitchOS TV installer is an independent customization project. It is not a replacement Android firmware, a CHiQ product, or an Apple-certified receiver. Product names identify compatible hardware, apps and protocols; they do not imply endorsement.

The repository contains the project's launcher source, its prebuilt APK, installer scripts, patch instructions and documentation. No CHiQ/Changhong firmware APK, extracted vendor library, decompiled vendor source, Android SDK, JDK, or signing key is distributed here.

The optional AirPlay build script works on a receiver APK supplied from the owner's TV. Its output remains local and ignored by Git. Ownership and licensing of that receiver remain with its respective authors. The script does not copy code from UxPlay; the documentation credits upstream research relevant to the diagnosis.

No open-source license has been selected for this initial publication. Public source visibility alone does not grant a broad redistribution license.
